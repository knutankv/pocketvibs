About the data
  • Linear is acceleration with gravity removed by iOS. +Gravity is accelerationIncludingGravity. The raw CSV has both, plus the rotation rate in °/s.
  • iOS Safari gives motion data at about 60 Hz, so the Nyquist limit is about 30 Hz. The timestamps are uneven, so the data is resampled onto an even grid at the measured mean rate before the PSD is computed.
  • The PSD matches scipy.signal.welch: periodic window and one-sided spectrum. Density gives units²/Hz and spectrum gives units².
